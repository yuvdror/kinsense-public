Looking for a Life360 alternative?

Most people who go looking are pushed rather than pulled — by the price, by the battery, or by finding out what happens to the location data. This page is the honest version of the comparison: what we do differently, what Life360 still does better, and where our own free tier is thinner than theirs.

Why people start looking

The data. In December 2021 The Markup reported that Life360 was selling precise location data to roughly a dozen data brokers, and that the company earned about $16 million from location data in 2020. Life360 has since said it stopped selling precise data to brokers. We have never sold location data to anyone, and there is no line item in our business that could.

The price. Life360’s published price for its Plus plan went from $24.99 to $49.99 a year in October 2025.

The teenager. A tracker the tracked person resents is a tracker that gets switched off, left at a friend’s house, or fed a spare phone. That is the problem we designed around.

What Life360 still does better

A comparison page that only flatters the author is worth nothing, so here is the other column.

  • It runs on Android today. TribeSentry is on iPhone. If half your family is on Android, Life360 is the app that works for them right now.
  • Its free history is deeper. Life360’s free plan keeps 2 days of location history. Ours keeps the last 2 hours unless you subscribe. Both free plans cap saved places at two.
  • It dispatches emergency services. On Life360’s paid plans, an unanswered crash alert escalates to a real dispatcher. Our crash detection alerts your tribe, and that is where it stops.
  • It bundles roadside assistance and identity protection. We do not sell insurance-style add-ons and have no plans to.

If emergency dispatch or roadside cover is the reason you pay, Life360 is the better buy and we would rather say so than win the click.

What ours costs

Everything that matters in an emergency is free: live location, SOS with the offline text fallback, crash detection, arrival and late alerts, battery and speed alerts, and the record of who checked on you. TribeSentry+ adds the full 7-day history with route playback, unlimited territories, offline map downloads, and removes ads. It is priced in your own currency in the app.

We charge for history depth because storing it is what actually costs us money. We do not charge for safety, because a paywall in front of an SOS button is the wrong product.

Switching

  1. Install TribeSentry and create a tribe.
  2. Invite the family by code, WhatsApp or text. Every person is approved by hand — an invite code is a request, not a key.
  3. Run both for a week. Then delete the old account rather than just uninstalling it, so the history goes with it.
