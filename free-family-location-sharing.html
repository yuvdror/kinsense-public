TribeSentry

Free family location sharing, without the data broker

“Free” in this category usually means one of three things: a trial, a crippled tier, or your family’s movements sold on to somebody you have never heard of. Here is exactly which parts of TribeSentry are free, which are not, and how we pay for it.

What is free

  • Live location for everyone in the tribe, on one map.
  • SOS. One tap alerts everyone with your exact position. With no data connection at all it falls back to a text message carrying your coordinates — a text gets through on a signal far too weak to load anything else.
  • Crash detection. If a phone detects what looks like a car crash, the tribe is told automatically, with the location. It is not a paid tier.
  • Arrival and late alerts. Get told when someone reaches Home or School, and when someone has not arrived by the time they normally would.
  • Two territories in every tribe, with arrive and leave alerts.
  • Battery and speed alerts, with a separate speed limit per person.
  • Who checked on you. Every time somebody opens your location, you see who and when.
  • Meeting point, dark mode, and 11 languages including right-to-left.

What is not free

TribeSentry+ is the optional upgrade. It adds the full 7-day location history with route playback, unlimited territories, offline map downloads for areas with no reception, and it removes ads. On the free tier the history window is the last 2 hours.

That split is deliberate, and it is the only one we think is defensible: storing history is the part that genuinely costs us money per user per month, so that is the part we charge for. Nothing that matters in an emergency is behind the paywall.

How the free tier is paid for

Ads, and people who upgrade. That is the whole list. We do not sell location data, we have never sold location data, and there is no arrangement under which anybody buys it from us. Location history is deleted automatically after 7 days, which also means there is very little to sell even if we changed our minds.

If an app is free and the company has no subscription worth speaking of, it is worth asking what is being sold instead. That question is the reason this app exists.

Free does not have to mean unsafe

A parent approves every single person who joins a tribe. Until then they see nothing at all — not the map, not the member list, not one location. Everyone chooses when to share and can stop at any moment, and the family is told sharing is off rather than being shown a stale pin that looks live. You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app.

Getting started

  1. Install the app and create a tribe.
  2. Invite people with a code, over WhatsApp, or by text.
  3. Approve each person as they ask to join.
